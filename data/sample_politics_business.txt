Government Announces New Fund to Support Small Businesses and Startups

Finance Minister Meera Kapoor announced a new support package for small businesses and startups in New Delhi on October 6, 2026. The government will create a Small Enterprise Growth Fund of Rs 5,000 crore, which will provide low-cost loans and credit guarantees to small manufacturers, shop owners and technology startups. Kapoor said the aim of the scheme is to create jobs and help local businesses compete in a changing global economy.

Under the new policy, businesses with an annual turnover of less than Rs 50 crore will be able to apply for loans of up to Rs 2 crore at reduced interest rates. The loans will be provided through public sector banks and will be monitored by the Reserve Bank of India. The government also said that the approval process will be fully online, and that applicants will receive a decision within fifteen working days. Officials believe the digital process will reduce paperwork and delays.

Industry groups reacted positively to the announcement. Sanjay Bhatia, president of the Confederation of Small Industries, said that many small firms struggle to get credit from banks because they lack collateral. He described the fund as a major step that could save thousands of businesses during a period of high costs. However, he asked the government to make sure that the scheme reaches smaller towns and not only big cities such as Mumbai and Bengaluru.

Opposition leaders criticised the plan. Congress leader Harish Verma argued that the amount is too small for a country with millions of small enterprises and said that earlier schemes had failed because of slow implementation. Kapoor replied in Parliament that the fund would be reviewed every six months and that the government would publish the results on a public website. She added that the budget for the scheme could be increased if demand was high.

Economists gave a mixed response. Dr. Neha Sharma, a senior economist at the National Institute of Economic Research, said the policy could increase employment in the manufacturing and services sectors if loans are given quickly. She also warned that banks must be careful to avoid a rise in unpaid loans. The scheme is expected to begin on January 1, 2027, and applications will open on a government portal two months earlier.
