Mumbai Strikers Beat Chennai Challengers in Last-Over Thriller

The Mumbai Strikers won the National T20 Trophy final by three wickets on Sunday, October 4, 2026, after a nail-biting last over at the Wankhede Stadium in Mumbai. Chasing a target of 168 runs, the Strikers needed 11 runs from the final six balls and completed the chase with just two balls to spare. A sold-out crowd of more than 30,000 fans watched captain Rohan Kulkarni hit the winning boundary.

The Chennai Challengers had earlier won the toss and chose to bat first. Opening batter Imran Qureshi played a brilliant innings of 72 runs from 48 balls, which included six fours and three sixes. However, the Challengers lost wickets regularly in the middle overs and managed only 167 runs for eight wickets. Strikers fast bowler Arjun Nair was the best bowler of the day, taking three wickets for 24 runs.

The chase did not begin well for the home team. The Strikers lost two early wickets inside the first five overs, and the pressure kept rising as the required run rate crossed ten runs per over. Kulkarni then built a strong partnership of 64 runs with young wicketkeeper Vikram Joshi. Joshi scored 41 runs and gave the team the momentum it needed before he was dismissed in the sixteenth over.

The match turned in the final over when Challengers captain Suresh Iyer gave the ball to spinner Karthik Raman. Kulkarni hit a six on the third ball and a four on the fifth ball to finish the game in front of a roaring home crowd. He ended the match with an unbeaten 58 runs from 39 balls and was named Player of the Match.

After the final, Kulkarni said the victory belonged to the whole team. He praised the young players for staying calm under pressure and thanked the fans for their support throughout the season. Challengers coach Dinesh Reddy congratulated the winners and said his team would learn from the loss. The Mumbai Strikers were presented with a trophy and prize money of Rs 2 crore by the Board of Cricket Administration. The club will now prepare for the Asian Club Championship, which begins in Dubai in January 2027.
